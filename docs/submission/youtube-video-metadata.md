## Title

EchoLock AI — A Safe Command Can Become Dangerous in Transit | 3-Minute Demo

## Description

A deep-space command can be safe when Earth sends it and dangerous when the
spacecraft receives it minutes later. EchoLock revalidates the command against
arrival-time reality, preserves its mission intent with a verified patch, and
proves the decision with a cryptographic Delta Certificate.

This three-minute demo shows:
- EXECUTE, ADAPT, DEFER, and REJECT decisions
- Force Original vs Reject Entirely vs EchoLock Verified
- immutable original-command escrow and an Intent-Safe Patch
- a SHA-256 Delta Certificate and hash-linked audit chain
- actual IBM Bob development evidence

Live demo: https://echolock-ai.onrender.com/
Source code: https://github.com/lsh2546/echolock-ai

Evaluation disclaimer: the displayed results come from 60 fixed, balanced,
deterministic synthetic PoC scenarios—15 per verdict. They are not estimates of
flight performance or operational accuracy. Battery, thermal, and communications
behavior are toy models.

IBM Bob was the principal AI development partner for the initial architecture and
core Phase 0/1 implementation. Codex independently audited and corrected Phase
1/1.1, then implemented Phase 2, the FastAPI demo, and submission preparation.
