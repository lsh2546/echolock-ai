## Rubric discrepancy

The Official Rules specify four scored criteria—Technical Execution, Innovation,
Challenge Fit, and Implementation & Feasibility—each 1–5, maximum 20. The Event
Platform page additionally lists Real-World Impact as a fifth criterion. Prepare
for all five, but treat the signed Official Rules as controlling.
